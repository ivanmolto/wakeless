Snapshot **2026-11-07**, generated by `pnpm leak:index` from the mainnet payment-channels program [`CHNLxYvVA28MJP9PrFuDXccuoGXAx7jBacfLEkahyGsX`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/CHNLxYvVA28MJP9PrFuDXccuoGXAx7jBacfLEkahyGsX), read-only through `mainnet.helius-rpc.com` (`getSignaturesForAddress`, `getTransaction`; no keypair loaded, nothing spent). Instructions decoded with the program's codama IDL (`vendor/payment-channels`, program 0.1.0): `open`, `settle`, `settleAndSeal`, `topUp`, `requestClose`, `seal`, `distribute`, `withdrawPayer` (the payer's refund) and `reclaim`. JSON: `docs/leak/snapshot-2026-11-07.json`.

**Sample:** 18,291 signatures scanned (cap 20,000, the program's whole history), block times 24 Jun 2026 to 7 Nov 2026; 18,291 transactions read, 94 of them failed on chain and counted for nothing. Every channel's `open` is inside the window. This is the public `batch-settlement` scheme as one facilitator runs it in preview; the numbers are the structure of the leak at this size, not a forecast.

Every address below is truncated to its first four and last four characters; the program id is the only full address. Aggregates and structure, not a directory of who pays whom.

**payTo** is the recipient with the largest share in the distribution the `open` fixes (the x402 `payTo`). The program's `payee` seat is not the vendor: the x402 SVM profile puts the facilitator's sponsor key there with a zero share (`vendor/x402/specs/schemes/upto/scheme_upto_svm.md`), and the sample shows 54 distinct payee seats across 2,464 channels, almost none of them a recipient.

## The `exact` scheme

The `exact` scheme pays each call with a direct token transfer, which names payer, payTo and amount in one instruction; it is the larger leak. This snapshot carries no row for it: x402scan documents no public export or API for Solana x402 transfers. Its Networks page showed 26,182 x402 transactions on Solana in the past 24 hours (5 facilitators, 428 sellers, 123 buyers) on 7 Nov 2026, not broken out by scheme, so the exact-scheme per-call transfer count is absent rather than estimated. Source checked: x402scan (Merit Systems), https://www.x402scan.com/networks and https://github.com/Merit-Systems/x402scan, read 7 Nov 2026.

## What Wakeless changes, and what it does not

A Wakeless channel puts none of this on the ledger: no channel account naming payer and payee, no voucher amount in an Ed25519 instruction, no payout per recipient. The watcher's `reconstruct()` recovers no vendor and no spend from a Wakeless run (`docs/WATCHER.md`, `docs/side-by-side.md`). The privacy claim is against the public ledger. Three operators see inside: the facilitator (vouchers), MagicBlock's enclave (private transfers) and NEAR's private shard (funding origin and route).
